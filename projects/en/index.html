Detail pages for the projects on my CV: my part, how each system works, the results and how to read them.

  • Hackathon · Oct 2026

    Phenikaa Campus Courier 2026 — Multimodal Delivery AI

    A system that reads a campus map image and a noisy Vietnamese instruction, then predicts the first move of a delivery robot. Built for the Phenikaa AI Hackathon 2026 qualifier, then continued into the final round.

  • NLP project · Oct–Nov 2026

    Vietnamese Address Normalization to the New Administrative Units after the 2025 Merger

    An offline, CPU-only system (no LLM, no external API) that turns free-form Vietnamese addresses into structured addresses under the administrative units in effect since 1 July 2025: 34 provinces, no district level.

  • Team project · Computer vision · Oct–Nov 2026

    Unsupervised Anomaly Detection and Localization on Industrial Products

    Three unsupervised methods (Autoencoder, PaDiM, PatchCore), implemented from scratch and compared under one protocol on 9 product categories from three public datasets, then stress-tested with five follow-up experiments.

  • Training program · Jul–Oct 2026

    Backend Training Program — Viettel Software

    Capstone of the program: a personal finance management system with an integrated AI agent. A TypeScript backend and web app, tested at several levels and started with a single Docker Compose command.

  • AI program capstone · Jun–Aug 2025

    Samsung Innovation Campus — Vietnam Traffic Sign Detection

    A team capstone for the Samsung Innovation Campus artificial intelligence program: real-time detection of Vietnamese traffic signs with YOLOv8, later extended by the team with tracking and a vision-language model.
