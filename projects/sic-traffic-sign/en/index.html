AI program capstone · Jun–Aug 2025

Samsung Innovation Campus — Vietnam Traffic Sign Detection

A team capstone for the Samsung Innovation Campus artificial intelligence program: real-time detection of Vietnamese traffic signs with YOLOv8, later extended by the team with tracking and a vision-language model.

Team of 3 · Python · PyTorch · Ultralytics YOLOv8

At a glance

  • YOLOv8mmedium model, moved up from the nano model
  • 25 → 100training epochs
  • 3team members

What it is

A pipeline that detects Vietnamese traffic signs in images and video using a YOLOv8-medium model trained on the Vietnam Traffic Signs dataset (Kaggle), in YOLO format. The repository has a training module, a prediction pipeline, a real-time version, a smoothed real-time version, and a hybrid version that adds a language model.

What I did

  • Trained and tuned the detector: from the nano model to YOLOv8-medium, from 25 to 100 epochs, and moved to a second version of the dataset.
  • Wrote the prediction pipeline and changed its input and output from text files to JSON.
  • Kept the training weights and configuration in the repository.

What the rest of the team did

  • SORT tracking and smoothed real-time output.
  • The Vintern-1B vision-language model, added after the main training work, to read sign text and directions, and later work on its dataset. This was done by teammates, mostly after the program ended.

How to read this

  • This is a student team project and the first machine learning project for all three of us. The repository does not publish a final accuracy figure, so I do not quote one here.
  • The team repository is hosted under a teammate's account; my commits there are mostly the training and prediction work from June 2025.
